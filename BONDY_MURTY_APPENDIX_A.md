# Bondy–Murty Appendix A (100 open problems) vs. this repo

Source: *Théorie des graphes* (Bondy, Murty; French edition by F. Havet, 2025),
Appendix A "Problèmes ouverts", book pp. 625–634 = PDF pp. 641–650 of
<https://inria.hal.science/hal-05211979v1> (file `master.pdf`).
This is the 100-problem list of the English *Graph Theory* (GTM 244, 2008), with
seven replacements made in the French edition (items 7, 10, 11, 46, 49, 77, 100)
for conjectures resolved since 2008 (Kelly, Ohba, Read unimodality,
Lovász–Plummer, Alon–Saks–Seymour, Steinberg, Hedetniemi).

Cross-check date: 2026-09-09. Method: keyword/regex search of every item over
`data/problems.json` (227 OPG records: title, statement, discussion, authors),
`data/arxiv_conjectures.json` (768 records), `data/erdos_graph.json`, and all
`*.md` under `problems/`, followed by manual reading of every near-match.

**Follow-up.** The 38 items marked **M** or **E** below were transcribed into a
third corpus, `data/bondy_murty_conjectures.json` (generated by
`scripts/bm_build_records.py`, ids `bm-NNN` = appendix number), reviewed with
`scraper/bm_review.py` into `data/bondy_murty_reviews/`, and rendered under
`/bm/<bm_id>/` on the site.
