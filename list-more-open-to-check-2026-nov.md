## arXiv:1507.01120 — Sparsity and dimension

- **Conjecture (Concluding Remarks)** (Joret, Micek, Ossona de Mendez, Wiechert; related: `AX_planar_cover_graph_poset_dimension_height`): Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a class of graphs closed under taking subgraphs. ## arXiv:1611.03196 — Fair representation by independent sets

<https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.03196>

- **Conjecture (Section 'Hypergraph versions')** (Alon, Drewnowski, Łuczak): For integers $n,k,s$ with $s\ge 2$ and $n>ks$, the chromatic number of the stable Kneser hypergraph $K(n,k,s)$ is $\lceil \frac{n-ks+s}{s-1}\rceil$. (Proved when $s$ is a power of 2.)

(Proved by Berger, Lo and Seymour for $G=K_{6,6}$.)
- **Ryser's conjecture on Latin squares (quoted in Section 1, Relationship to known conjectures)** (Ryser): Every Latin square of odd order $n$ has a transversal, that is, a set of $n$ entries from distinct rows and distinct columns containing distinct symbols.

- **Conjecture 1.12** (Aharoni, Barát, Wanless; related: `AX_fair_representation_bipartite_matching_under_representation`): If $E_1,E_2,\ldots,E_m$ are sets of edges in a bipartite graph and $|E_i|>\Delta\left(\bigcup_{i\le m}E_i\right)+1$ for every $i$, then there exists a rainbow matching, that is, a matching consisting of one edge from each $E_i$ (distinct edges for distinct $i$). (The bound $\Delta+1$ does not suffice, and the statement fails for multisets.)
- **remark in Section 1 (intersection of two matroids)** (Aharoni, Alon, Berger, Chudnovsky, Kotlar, Loebl, Ziv (remark: no counterexample known)): For any two matroids $\mathcal M$ and $\mathcal N$ on the same ground set, is $\beta(\mathcal M\cap\mathcal N)\le\max(\beta(\mathcal M),\beta(\mathcal N))+1$? (The paper says no instance violating this is known to the authors.)
